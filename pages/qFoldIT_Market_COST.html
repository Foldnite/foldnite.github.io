INTERPRETATION

Why $235M — not $4B — today?

The technology and team are unusually valuable, but the largest strategic premium is still an option value. The $1–4B band requires proof of commercial network effects rather than only architecture.

  • Current value is driven by rare team composition + technology breadth + platform architecture + Tauri/Rust enterprise productization + live platform metrics (608+ tools, Industrial CAMEO, Evidence Levels, CI/CD).
  • Enterprise revenue and scientific outcomes are the primary missing valuation evidence.
  • Creator/Academy/marketplace economics are designed and can become material, but the supplied file explicitly positions them as planning/roadmap layers.
  • The correct valuation method is therefore a blend of strategic asset value + option value, not a pure ARR multiple.
