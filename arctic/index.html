Digital twin of MAS «Snowflake» — reality accurate to a centimetre

A game built on the digital twin of the International Scientific Station «Snowflake». A virtual copy constructed from photogrammetry and geodetic scanning data lets you experience the real operation of the scientific equipment of the member countries of the project. Every detail — from ice structure to module layout — is recreated with an accuracy of 1 to 3 centimetres.

The project is implemented as an import-independent digital twin for the VK Play platform. We combined industrial UAVs with 8K sensors, professional-grade panoramic cameras and satellite imagery from the «Kanopus-V» constellation. Geodetic referencing via GNSS receivers (GLONASS) in RTK mode provided sub-decimetre terrain accuracy. The result is a complete copy of the area, where every micro-relief feature affects the physics of all-terrain vehicles and snowbikes and the calculation of signal coverage zones.

Dynamic weather and day-night cycle — blizzards, polar night, the northern lights — all of this changes the gameplay and demands adaptation.

🪐 Key station zones

1. Energy Zone (Core Energy)

The heart of «Snowflake». The player balances generation and consumption across the whole station.

  • Gameplay: Tuning electrolysers during strong winds, switching to fuel cells in calm weather, activating backup diesel generators in emergencies.
  • Equipment: Wind farm, solar farm, electrolysers, inverters, hydrogen storage systems, backup diesel generators.

2. Climate & Environment Zone

Real-time data from the Arctic environment — the station's eyes and ears.

  • Gameplay: Deploying permafrost sensors, launching weather balloons, analysing atmospheric composition, predicting storms from live charts.
  • Equipment: Lidars, gas analysers, permafrost sensors, meteorological stations.

3. Biology & Medicine Zone

Closed-loop life sciences and crew health — from extremophile proteins to telemedicine.

  • Gameplay: Protein folding of cold-resistant enzymes, growing plants in aeroponics/hydroponics, VR medical consultations.
  • Equipment: Bioreactors, automated plant systems, microscopes, incubators, telemedicine consoles.
Automated plant system — Biology & Medicine zone

4. Robotics & Transport Zone

The most dynamic part of the station — drones, rovers and the entire vehicle fleet.

  • Gameplay: Remote control of drones and all-terrain vehicles, route planning on micro-relief, programming AI for autonomous snow clearing.
  • Equipment: UAV consoles, robotic manipulators, drone port, warm hangar, helipad.

5. Materials & Engineering Zone

Testing and prototyping new materials in extreme Arctic conditions.

  • Gameplay: Testing composites in climate chambers, printing spare parts on-site, running virtual test benches.
  • Equipment: 3D printers, CNC machines, climate chambers, structural test rigs.

6. Connectivity & Control Zone

In the Arctic, connectivity is survival — and the Central Control Post is the station's brain.

  • Gameplay: Deploying Mesh networks, configuring satellite terminals, fighting radio interference during the northern lights, managing all station systems from a single dashboard.
  • Equipment: Satellite terminals, server racks, control consoles, quantum-safe communication channels.
Connectivity Hub satellite terminals

7. Living & Life Support Zone

Everyday life and closed-loop life support for the crew of 80.

  • Gameplay: Balancing crew rest and workload, managing closed water and air cycles, waste sorting, running the laundry.
  • Equipment: Crew quarters, canteen, gym, coworking, reverse-osmosis filters, air regeneration units, waste sorting line.

🚜 Digital vehicle fleet (Arctic Fleet) — multiplayer

The digital twin lets you test the movement physics of every vehicle type. Multiplayer is available in vehicle mode — explore the station together with friends.

  • All-terrain vehicles and snowmobiles: Transporting cargo and scientists between modules over rough terrain, taking real micro-relief into account.
  • Aerial drones: Terrain photogrammetry, monitoring the condition of the station domes.
  • Underwater drones (AUV/ROV): Exploring the under-ice space, inspecting bottom sensors, hydrological research in icy water.

Ultra-precise terrain (Digital Terrain)

1–3 cm accuracy opens up unique possibilities:

  • Route planning: Take every bump and crack into account so as not to overturn an all-terrain vehicle or lose a drone.
  • Geodesy: Virtual marking of the territory for installing new scientific instruments.
  • Design: The ability to plan your own rooms and laboratories right in virtual reality.
Laser scanning (lidar) — the basis of sub-decimetre terrain accuracy

🎯 Game loop: an example task

The player receives a task: «Install a weather station at a remote location». You must:

  1. Choose a suitable all-terrain vehicle in the garage.
  2. Check the battery charge (interaction with the energy zone).
  3. Plot a route on the high-precision map.
  4. On site, set up a stable communication channel with the base (connectivity zone).
  5. In case of a breakdown — send a drone for inspection (robotics zone).

Every action requires an understanding of how real equipment works and affects the overall success of the mission.

📡 Creation technologies

We used a combination of advanced methods:

  • Industrial UAVs with 8K sensors for detailed surveying.
  • Panoramic cameras of professional grade.
  • Satellite imagery from the «Kanopus-V» constellation for orthophoto maps.
  • GNSS receivers (GLONASS) in RTK mode — geodetic referencing accurate to the centimetre.
The project is fully import-independent and implemented on the VK Play platform. VK Play users get exclusive access to the VR, PC and WEB versions, as well as custom levels from the developers. Multiplayer is available in all-terrain vehicle and other vehicle modes.

Educational and R&D game environment: 9 principles of the Sirius «World of Knowledge»

The architecture of the MAS «Snowflake» digital twin was designed around 9 foundational principles of an educational game environment — from accessibility on any device to evidence-based scientific validation via Scientific MCP.

01 · ACCESSIBILITY

Entry levels and multi-platform

Scenarios of different difficulty (from schoolchild to engineer), clear instructions and a choice of participation via PC, VR or WEB with no geographic restrictions.

02 · FIRST-CAUSE THINKING

Physics and logic instead of memorisation

The model visibly demonstrates cause-and-effect relationships: why the system works this way, which physical constraints apply, and what each parameter affects.

03 · SOCIAL LEARNING

Team roles and hypothesis exchange

Multiplayer with distributed roles of researcher, engineer, analyst and coordinator, requiring joint decision-making and hypothesis testing.

04 · ENGAGEMENT THROUGH PLAY

Meaningful goals without replacing science

Game tasks, clear progress and feedback hold attention, but game score never substitutes for scientific correctness (validated through WorkflowMCP).

05 · BELONGING TO SOMETHING BIGGER

Real Arctic challenges

Every educational action is tied to a major scientific task — operating an autonomous hydrogen-powered Arctic station, climatology and polar robotics.

06 · RESPONSIBILITY THROUGH CONSEQUENCES

Systemic effect of every decision

The model shows the real consequences of mistakes and discoveries for the energy balance, rover logistics, crew safety, resources and the fragile ecosystem.

07 · INDEPENDENT PATH CHOICE

Freedom of method and captured logic

The participant chooses their role, sequence of steps and acceptable solution method themselves, while the system records their trajectory and justification in the Mission Contract.

08 · LIVING ADAPTIVE ENVIRONMENT

Tuning with a mentor, not a "black box"

Mission updates and difficulty levels are built on collaboration with a teacher and feedback, not opaque automatic profiling.

09 · EXPERIENCE, NOT MEMORISATION

Research, solution and debrief

The full experience cycle: independent modelling, decision-making in a dynamic environment, team discussion and a structured debrief based on the validation report.

Scientific MCP · 20 research servers

The digital twin of MAS «Snowflake» on UNIGINE 2 is an extreme testbed for applied research and engineering R&D. Through the standardised Scientific MCP protocol the simulation links to 20 specialised compute servers, grouped into 4 scientific families and one validation platform WorkflowMCP.

BIOMCP · 6 SERVERS Biotech & Medicine

Molecular biology and extremophiles

Servers: Protein/Ligand · RNA/Protein · Protein Design · Protein/Pathway · Enzyme/Protein · Protein/Microbe.

Tasks & industries: Pharma, RNA therapy, AI-drug discovery, synthetic and industrial biotech, agritech in the Arctic phytolab.
MATERIALSMCP · 5 SERVERS Materials & Hydrogen

Crystals, catalysts and energy storage

Servers: Crystal/Molecular · Crystal Lattice · Catalyst/MOF · MOF/Adsorption · Crystal/Ligand.

Tasks & industries: Chemistry, frost-resistant batteries, hydrogen storage and carbon capture (CCS), catalysis, mining.
TOPOMCP · 5 SERVERS Physics & Microelectronics

Topology, photonics and quantum systems

Servers: Node/Crystal · Topology Graph · 3D Topology · Optical/Topology · Quantum Topology.

Tasks & industries: Semiconductors, AI hardware, non-volatile memory, lithography, optical comms and quantum computing.
ENGMCP · 3 SERVERS Engineering & CAD

CAD modelling and structural physics

Servers: CAD/Material · CAD/Crystal · CAD/Physics.

Tasks & industries: Aerospace, UAVs and Arctic rovers, automotive, scientific software and virtual structural testing.
⚙️ WORKFLOWMCP · ORCHESTRATION AND VALIDATION LAYER (1 CORE)
Mission Contract → SHA-256 → Independent validator

Infrastructure layer on top of all four scientific domains. It records the mission contract, hashes input data (SHA-256), isolates runs for reproducibility, and routes candidates to an independent validator, producing a signed report.

Scientific integrity principle: gameplay score in «Snowflake» does not replace scientific results. None of the 19 domain servers self-certifies — every solution is accepted through the independent WorkflowMCP validation loop.

AI assistant: Yandex AI Studio & GitVerse

Virtual labs of MAS «Snowflake» (PC / VR / WEB) integrate the YandexGPT models through the official OpenAI-compatible Yandex AI Studio API. The neural network works together with 20 Scientific MCP servers and the GitVerse platform: it translates natural-language hypotheses into structured engineering formats, eliminating hallucinations through mandatory computational validation.

01 · REQUEST IN UNIGINE 2

Hypothesis in natural language

A researcher in VR or browser describes desired properties of a molecule, material or UAV flight mission.

02 · YANDEX AI STUDIO

Structured Output & Tools

YandexGPT converts text into strict JSON (SMILES, crystal lattice parameters or an MCP tool call).

03 · SCIENTIFIC MCP + 3D

Computation and visualisation

BioMCP / MaterialsMCP / EngMCP servers validate physics, return 3D coordinates to UNIGINE 2 and a WorkflowMCP report.

04 · GITVERSE & TEAM

Branching and review

Successful versions (Version A / Version B) are committed to GitVerse with a SHA-256 hash for joint review and merge in multiplayer.
